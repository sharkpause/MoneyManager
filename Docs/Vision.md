# Money Manager Vision

## Purpose

Money Manager exists to help me understand what actually happened financially.

The goal is not simply to track transactions, budgets, investments, or net worth. The goal is to preserve the meaning of financial events so that reports, statistics, and insights accurately reflect reality.

---

## Audience

This project is built strictly for **me and me only**.

Aside from hosting the source code in a public GitHub repository, it will never be deployed or operated as a product for anyone else.

Every design choice, workflow, assumption, and trade-off should be unapologetically selfish—tailored directly to how I think about money and how I manage my own finances, with zero optimization for a broader audience.

---

## Problem

Most personal finance applications reduce financial activity into simplistic income and expense categories.

This causes many different financial events to be represented incorrectly:

* Investments become transfers or expenses.
* Reimbursements become income.
* Transfers appear as spending.
* Market fluctuations pollute cash-flow reports.
* Financial reports become distorted.

As a result, I lose trust in the generated insights.

---

## Core Principle

Every financial event must preserve its objective financial meaning.

The system should record what actually happened, rather than forcing events into categories that are convenient for reporting.

Meaning must never be sacrificed for simplicity.

---

## Desired Outcome

At any point in time, I should be able to open the application and immediately understand my financial situation with confidence.

The reports generated by the application should be truthful, understandable, and useful for both long-term financial analysis and honest monthly self-checks on my spending discipline.

---

## Primary Use Case

Historical analysis and personal accountability.

The application is primarily designed to answer questions about what happened financially over time—including whether I was disciplined with my spending in a given month, whether I stayed under or went over target, and what habits I should adjust.

Forecasting, complex budgeting systems, and other features are secondary to accurate historical truth, though reports should support debt projection views where obligations exist.

---

## Information Philosophy

The application should be simple by default and detailed when requested.

I should be able to quickly understand high-level information and progressively drill down into categories, transactions, fees, notes, and historical context.

---

## Data Ownership

My financial data belongs to me.

The system prioritizes local-first and offline-first principles whenever practical.

I should always be able to access, export, and analyze my financial data independently of any external service provider.

---

## Non-Goals

Money Manager is not intended to be:

* A product or SaaS for a broader audience
* A social finance platform
* A gamified budgeting application
* An AI-powered financial assistant
* Business accounting software
* A generalized tool designed around hypothetical users' workflows

The system is designed exclusively for my own personal financial understanding and accurate historical analysis.

---

## Success Criteria

The project succeeds if it allows me to understand what actually happened with my money, keeps my spending discipline honest, and provides financial insights that I completely trust.
